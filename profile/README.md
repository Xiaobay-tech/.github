**From Vision to Devices.**

Xiaobay-tech is a technology organization focused on computer vision, on-device development, and embedded systems. We share engineering practice, reusable components, and developer tools.

- **Computer vision:** detection, recognition, YOLO, model training, and deployment.
- **On-device development:** Android, C++, multimedia processing, and high-performance inference.
- **Embedded systems:** edge devices, integration, and reusable open-source tools.
